## 1.0.0 — unreleased

First release. Offline, account-free car maintenance: service reminders due by
distance *and* date, fuel tracking with real consumption, an odometer log, trips
and trip expenses, the running cost of ownership, full service history and more
than one vehicle.

Six languages — English, Deutsch, Français, فارسی, العربية and کوردیی ناوەندی —
in both reading directions.

No account, no server, no network code of any kind. The backup is a plain file
you keep.
