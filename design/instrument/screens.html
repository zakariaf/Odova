No account. No sign-up.
No server. Nothing is uploaded.
No tracking, no analytics, no ads.

Odova has no way to reach the internet and never asks for it. Everything you enter stays on this phone until you export it yourself.

If you lose the phone without a backup, the records are gone — that is the trade for keeping them private.
