## The verdict, and why it is that one

EPIC-19 depends on this file, and the honest answer is that the visual work is
not finished. Signing it would make the next epic's first assumption false.

`design-review-workflow` rule 5 makes a contrast miss a mandatory BLOCKER, and
there is none: EPIC-17 closed the finding, moved seven token values, re-shot all
112 references and left `knownContrastExceptions` empty with a test that keeps
it empty. That is the one gate this sign-off can honestly clear.

Three things stop the rest.

**1. The band profile fails on 106 of 112 comparisons, median 53%.** The
difference is vertical rhythm and it is real — on `settings` the app's first
card starts 16 px lower than the reference's and every band below inherits the
offset. What is NOT established is the cause, per screen or in general. It
cannot be established without the pass named below.

**2. Task 18.8's human side-by-side pass was not done.** The sweep produced 112
sheets and **four** were read: `settings-light-ltr`, `home-light-ltr`,
`log.fillup-light-ltr` and `vehicles-light-rtl`. Those four produced two
BLOCKERs, nine FIXes and five NOTEs, and **not one of them was found by the
three automated checks.** Extrapolating that rate over 108 unread sheets is the
whole reason this file says NOT SIGNED rather than "mostly fine".

**3. Task 18.9's structured pass on a release build was not run at all.** It
needs a device or an emulator and this epic had neither. `design/review/shots/`
therefore does not exist — it is not an empty directory and not a set of debug
captures relabelled, because a matrix shot on the wrong build is worse than a
missing one.

## What this sweep did establish

| | First run | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Comparisons | 112 | 112 |
| Passing all three checks | 6 | 6 |
| Failing the band profile | 106 | 106 |
| Painting an untokenised surface | 17 | 11 |
| Built in the wrong theme | 2 | 0 |
| Median band-edge miss | 59% | 53% |

Five defects were found by putting all 28 screens in one run, and every one of
them had been passing its own per-screen parity test for four epics:

- **Twelve artboards draw a back arrow and no screen in the app drew one.** Ten
  screens now use `CalmAppBar.pushed`. A pushed screen whose only way out was
  an Android system gesture is a §17 traversal failure as much as a visual one.
- **`home` never drew its overdue count pill** — the only element that answers
  the question §9 says the screen exists for, before a card is read. Fixed.
- **The odometer field did not group its digits on blur**, because §10's
  sentence had a function with tests and no callers. Fixed.
- **`costs.fuel` is missing most of its reference content** — the unit label,
  the tank-count line, the This-tank/Best/Worst figures, the range chip, both
  axis labels, the legend and the whole price trio. NOT fixed; the widget has
  no code for any of it. This is an unfinished screen, and it is the largest
  single item left.
- **Dark modal screens paint pure black under the scrim** where the composite
  should be `#0F0C0A`. NOT fixed.

Two of the checks were themselves wrong and are corrected, neither by widening a
tolerance: the capture drew no phone chrome where every artboard has a status
bar and a home indicator, and the colour census read `#RRGGBB` while `--scrim`
is an `rgba()`, so every modal screen's composited backdrop was reported as
untokenised. `--token-tolerance` and `--band-tolerance` are untouched and no
reference was regenerated.

## What must happen before this can be signed

1. Read the remaining 108 sheets. Four produced sixteen findings.
2. Establish the band class's cause and close it, or record a deliberate design

3. Build `costs.fuel` to its reference.
4. Run `design-review-workflow`'s four lenses on a release build at the largest
   text scale, and produce `design/review/shots/`.
5. Fix F-8 (`#000000` under the dark scrim) or explain it.
